Development Priorities
Phase 1: Identity
Build the CLI, authentication, username registry, and profiles.

Phase 2: Social
Introduce discovery, chat rooms, messaging, and personal notes.

Phase 3: Hero Feature
Develop the connector marketplace, publishing workflow, permissions, and agent tool integration.

Phase 4: Expansion
Introduce business accounts, local USB integration, secure cloud computing, Web3, and mobile access.

Success criterion: A new user can register, claim a handle, meet another user, install a connector, and perform a useful action through their agent.

Technical distinction: ENS .eth names can associate wallet addresses and conventional email contact records, but don't automatically provide working email inboxes. Email provisioning requires a separate service. W3C DIDs are another identity mechanism.
