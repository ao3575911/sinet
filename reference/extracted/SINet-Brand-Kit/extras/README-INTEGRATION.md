# Integration quickstart

1. Web: reference `logos/svg/primary-dark.svg` on dark surfaces and `logos/svg/primary-light.svg` on light surfaces.
2. App: use `icons/app-icon-dark-1024.png` for marketing, `icons/favicon.ico` and `icons/site.webmanifest` on the web. Move referenced webmanifest files into your site's public `/icons/` folder.
3. Social: export and upload each appropriately sized image in `social/`.
4. UI: import `tokens/sinet.css`.
5. CLI: `extras/terminal-splash.txt` is brand copy, not executable shell code.
6. PNG files are transparent where appropriate; SVG wordmarks are paths and don't need a font installed.
7. Keep the mark's geometry unchanged. Minimum clearspace around mark: at least 15% of its height. Avoid stretch, recoloring, unapproved gradients, or placing the dark wordmark on a light background.
8. Fonts are not distributed. Free use of Inter must follow the font's own open-source license when downloaded separately.
9. Validate SINet naming, trademark and usernames before public rollout. No exclusivity or clearance implied.
