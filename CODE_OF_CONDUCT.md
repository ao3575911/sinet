# Community expectations

Treat people respectfully. Terminal experience, identity and background do not determine
who belongs. Give concrete, constructive feedback; respect consent, privacy and boundaries.

Harassment, threats, impersonation, doxxing, spam and deliberate publication of private
credentials or data are unacceptable. Do not manipulate engagement or fabricate product
capabilities. Follow the security policy when reporting vulnerabilities.

Maintainers may edit/remove disruptive content and restrict participation, recording the
reason and considering context. Request a private maintainer channel for sensitive conduct
reports; do not post the affected person's private information publicly. This project does
not advertise a staffed support service or a guaranteed response schedule.
